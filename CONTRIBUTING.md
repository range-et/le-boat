# Contributing to Le Boat

Contributions are welcome in CAD, CFD, fabrication, sails, electronics, testing and documentation.
Use [repository issues](https://github.com/range-et/le-boat/issues) to discuss tasks.
Submit changes through a pull request.

## Before you start

Read the [design brief](design-brief.md) and [tool plan](project/tools-and-optimization.md).
Check the [decision register](project/decisions.md) for open questions.
Describe the design question and proposed change in an issue.
Keep the work small enough to review.

## Required records

- CAD: editable source, units, parameter values, revision IDs and suitable exports.
- CFD: geometry revision, complete case settings, convergence evidence and result data.
- Fabrication: materials, settings, measured masses and assembly notes.
- Tests: procedure, conditions, acceptance criteria, measurements, uncertainty and failures.
- Code: a reproducible example and checks appropriate to the change.
- AI use: significant contributions and completed checks from the journal template.

For physical changes, describe effects on draft, mass, stability, seals and structural loads.
Use git-cad for CAD management when its required workflow is available.
Use Hawa for CFD and Monad System for website UI.
Record unavailable functions and temporary manual procedures.

## Licenses

Submit only material that you have authority to license.
Contributions for inclusion use the applicable terms in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).
Keep third-party notices.
You retain copyright in your contribution.
Separate commercial permission can require agreement from each relevant rights holder.
The maintainer receives no automatic right to sell licenses for your contribution.

Exclude proprietary plans, copied geometry, credentials and private conversations unless suitable permission applies.
Cite reference designs and identify their sources.

## Documentation and website

Use short, direct English with ASD-STE100 sentence structure.
Keep necessary technical terms and explain uncommon terms.
Avoid slogans, marketing claims and contrast phrases such as “not X, but Y”.
Keep standard third-party license texts unchanged.

Project Markdown files supply the website reference pages.
Follow the build steps in [README.md](README.md).
Include generated page changes in the pull request.
Date journal entries and label planned work separately from completed work.
