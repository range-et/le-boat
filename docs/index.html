The place sets the problem

A heart-shaped pond.
A shallow-water brief.

Love Park is our intended sailing spot: a small urban water feature at Queens Quay West and York Street. The ambition is simple: carry a boat from home, catch a little wind, and share the water considerately.

20–60 cmPublished pond depth
≈ 8–24 inches

That depth makes the keel, ballast and stability the first real puzzle. We’re investigating a total loaded draft of 150 mm or less. Actual clearance, sailing access and a recovery route still need checking.

The pieces of the puzzle

A boat is a whole system.

Inspired by small RC yachts. Sized around the pond, the printer, and the weight of everything aboard.

01

Float & stay upright

Hull volume, mass budget, shallow ballast and rig size have to agree. A shortened keel needs a fresh stability check.

02

Make & mend

Rhino CAD, A1 mini print sections, sealed joints and small reinforcement trials before a complete hull.

03

Catch & control wind

Cloth sails do the moving. Battery-powered radio and servos steer and trim. Recovery needs its own plan.
