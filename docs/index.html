Design brief and research

RC sailboat for Love Park

Le Boat is an experimental model yacht for Love Park in Toronto. Wind provides all propulsion. Radio-controlled servos steer the rudder and adjust the sails.

We will design the boat in Rhino, print parts at home, compare flow with Hawa, and test the completed assembly.

Sailing site

Love Park is at Queens Quay West and York Street. Waterfront Toronto lists a pond depth of 20–60 cm, approximately 8–24 inches.

We propose a loaded draft of 150 mm or less. Route depths, clearance, recovery access and operating rules need checks before sailing.

Design optimization

Compare hull and appendage designs against a recorded baseline. Keep physical conditions fixed for each comparison.

  • Reduce drag at defined speeds and attitudes.
  • Provide the required lateral force and stability.
  • Meet draft, mass and fabrication limits.
  • Check selected designs with physical tests.
