I’m building a small, wind-powered RC sailboat for Love Park in Toronto. This site records the design, printing, assembly and sailing as the project develops.

It’s a collaborative project. Ideas, shared experiments and contributions are welcome.

The boat

A small RC yacht with cloth sails, a keel, ballast and a rudder. Wind provides propulsion. Radio-controlled servos handle steering and sail trim.

I’m using a Bambu A1 mini. Printed parts and hull sections will fit its 180 × 180 × 180 mm build volume.

The shallow pond affects the keel and rudder depth. Dimensions, equipment and the construction method are still open.

Tools and collaboration

I’m using Rhino and Grasshopper for geometry, git-cad for CAD management, Hawa for CFD and Monad System for this website.

AI tools are part of the process. Codex helped with research, documentation and the website.

Suggestions and contributions are welcome through GitHub.
