001 — Initial design brief

November 8, 2026 · Phase: brief and research

The owner proposed a small RC yacht inspired by the DragonForce 65. Home fabrication uses an A1 mini, with possible glass and resin reinforcement. Love Park is the intended sailing site. The project also studies how people and AI tools can design and build a physical object together.

The initial brief specifies wind propulsion and battery-powered radio controls. Google Maps identified the location. Waterfront Toronto's fact sheet lists a pond depth of 20–60 cm. This range makes shallow draft an important design requirement. Actual route depths and operating arrangements need checks.

The owner selected noncommercial reuse with attribution and separate commercial permission. The project established separate licenses for code and CAD/documentation. The first website and build record accompanied the brief. The proposed 350–450 mm hull length and 150 mm loaded draft require further study. Boat CAD, material purchases, fabrication samples, CFD and water tests remained pending.

AI use: Codex helped with research, documentation and website code. The owner supplied the project intent and commercial licensing requirement. The record contains no completed hull review or physical test.

Next tasks: check site access, measure equipment masses and compare shallow ballast arrangements. The original “75oz fiberglass” specification remains unresolved.
