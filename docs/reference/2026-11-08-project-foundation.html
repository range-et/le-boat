001 — Starting the boat

November 8, 2026 · Early design

I’m starting a small RC sailboat for Love Park in Toronto. The DragonForce 65 is an initial reference for the layout. Wind will provide propulsion, with radio controls for steering and sail trim.

I’m using a Bambu A1 mini, so the printed parts will fit its build volume. I’m also considering fiberglass and resin reinforcement, possibly with vacuum bagging. The construction method and material choices are still open.

The pond’s published depth is 20–60 cm. That makes the keel depth and ballast arrangement important early choices. Next I’ll explore the hull layout and estimate the equipment weight.

This is also a collaborative experiment with AI tools. Codex helped with the initial research, notes and website. Boat CAD, prints, CFD cases and water tests are still ahead.
