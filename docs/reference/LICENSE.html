Read the standard licenses for their definitions and exceptions. CC NonCommercial concerns commercial advantage or monetary compensation. An activity can fall outside that grant even when it makes no profit. PolyForm expressly permits certain organization uses. Its definitions and permissions differ from CC. License choice and limits

Creative Commons recommends software-specific licenses for software. Le Boat therefore separates code from design and documentation. MIT, Apache and CERN OHL permit commercial reuse. Those licenses would not reserve commercial permission as the owner requested. CC ShareAlike applies to shared content adaptations under its terms. The selected software license has separate conditions.

The licenses apply only where relevant legal rights exist. Functional features, manufactured objects and AI-assisted output can raise questions about copyright, design rights and other protection. CC grants no patent or trademark rights. An NC license cannot guarantee control over every physical boat or use of an idea. Obtain legal review before relying on these terms for commercial manufacture or enforcement.

Both licenses contain warranty and liability terms. See DISCLAIMER.md for the project notice. These terms apply only as far as the law allows. They cannot guarantee that no liability arises.
