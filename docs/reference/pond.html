Love Park

Love Park is at Queens Quay West and York Street in Toronto. It is the intended sailing spot for this boat.

Waterfront Toronto’s fact sheet lists the pond depth as 20–60 cm, approximately 8–24 inches. The shallow water affects the keel, rudder and ballast design. Water level and obstacles will affect the clearance available on the day.

The project page describes the heart-shaped pond and central island. The site’s current conditions and model-sailing arrangements still need a check.
