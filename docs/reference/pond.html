Love Park: site research

Research recorded November 8, 2026. Published descriptions are background evidence, not an on-site survey or permission to operate.

Location and water

  • Google Maps: Love Park was checked directly and identifies 96 Queens Quay W, Toronto. It is useful for location and access context, not bathymetry.
  • Waterfront Toronto's official fact sheet gives 20–60 cm pond depth (about 8–24 inches). The initial description of an 18-foot deep end conflicts with that source and is not a design input.
  • Waterfront Toronto's project page describes the heart-shaped pond and central island. These make turning space and retrieval paths relevant.
  • City of Toronto park listing locates the park at York Street and Queens Quay West.

Treat depth as spatially variable. The design must consider keel, ballast and rudder together, plus heel, trim, debris and clearance under the lowest point. Published depth is not a guarantee of current fill level or an unobstructed sailing area.

Operating status remains unconfirmed

Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 608, section 608-19 addresses powered models in parks and permit requirements. We have not established how the operator applies that provision to a wind-propelled model with radio servos. Do not infer an exemption from the absence of a propulsion motor. Confirm with the City/park operator, check posted rules and closures, and establish suitable launch/retrieval arrangements before trials. No operator has been contacted as part of this research.

First site visit

Record date, fill condition and any posted restrictions. Ask the operator about model sailing, RC controls, seasonal filling, maintenance and inaccessible areas. With permission where needed, measure depths along a short shore-accessible route and mark obstructions, intake/outlet areas and the island. Record the measurement method and uncertainty; do not derive depths from satellite colour.

Observe wind shifts and dead spots around trees and buildings, without claiming a wind survey from a single visit. Identify a launch point and recovery reach from dry land. Avoid routes that need wading, climbing onto the pond edge, or stepping into planting. Keep public paths clear and stop around wildlife or crowded edges.

Deliverable: an annotated site sketch with measured depth points, usable route, excluded areas, shore recovery positions and the operating confirmation. Until then, the 150 mm draft goal is provisional.
