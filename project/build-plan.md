# Next build steps

November 8, 2026 · Early design

The next work is the first hull layout and an equipment mass estimate.
That will help me choose the boat’s size, ballast arrangement and rig.

I’m designing around the A1 mini’s build volume and the pond’s shallow water.
Early CAD work will explore hull sections, joints, equipment access and keel depth.
Small printed samples will help with fit, sealing and any fiberglass reinforcement.

As the geometry develops, I’ll use Hawa to compare flow around the hull and appendages.
The journal will record the model, settings and results for each experiment.
I’ll also record finished weights, assembly changes and what happens during float and sailing tests.

These are working notes for my build.
The sequence and design choices can change as I learn more.
Suggestions and shared experiments are welcome.
