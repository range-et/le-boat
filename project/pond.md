# Love Park

Love Park is at Queens Quay West and York Street in Toronto.
It is the intended sailing spot for this boat.

[Waterfront Toronto’s fact sheet](https://www.waterfrontoronto.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Love%20Park%20Fact%20Sheet_0.pdf) lists the pond depth as **20–60 cm**, approximately **8–24 inches**.
The shallow water affects the keel, rudder and ballast design.
Water level and obstacles will affect the clearance available on the day.

The [project page](https://www.waterfrontoronto.ca/our-projects/love-park) describes the heart-shaped pond and central island.
The site’s current conditions and model-sailing arrangements still need a check.
