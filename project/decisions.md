## Decisions before detailed hull design

1. Verified sailing route, depth and recovery/access arrangements.
2. Equipment shortlist, measured mass, budget and battery endurance target.
3. Displacement, beam, ballast mass/lever arm, appendage architecture and rig size.
4. Filament, shell/joint design and whether the print is structure or tooling.
5. Intended fiberglass weight and units, resin and bagging method if used.
6. Acceptance criteria for heel, knockdown recovery, wind range and control endurance.

Record each decision with alternatives, reasons, evidence, date and affected revisions.
Keep provisional values labelled until the required checks pass.
